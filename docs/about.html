About This App

A 3x3 cube puzzle simulator that runs entirely in your browser. No install, no app store — just open this page and start playing right away.

It supports a wide range of skill levels, from a step-by-step tutorial (LBL method) for those who don't know how to solve a cube, to analysis tools for advanced solvers looking to shave time off their solves.

What You Can Do

  • Intuitive controls — just swipe with your finger
  • Learn the fundamentals through a step-by-step tutorial (supports 9 languages)
  • Register any sequence of moves as a "command" and replay it with a double-tap
  • Automatic solving via an external solver
  • Time tracking and rewind/replay of your move history
  • Net (unfolded) diagram view, and free control of the camera angle

About This App's Safety

  • We put your enjoyment first: this app shows no ads whatsoever, and we have no plans to introduce any in the future
  • The only external communication this app makes is with a solver service that computes cube solutions. All that's sent is a string representing the cube's state — no name, device information, usage data, or any other personally identifying information is ever sent
  • Information stored only in your browser (localStorage) is limited to the following. All of it stays on your own device and is never sent anywhere:
  • Settings such as speed, language, axis display, and command bindings
  • High scores (recorded times)
  • Tutorial progress
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind is used
